Summary

Every page and file on universitybikeshop.com. The site has nine public pages, which appear in the navigation bar and the sitemap, and five unlisted pages under /secret/, including this one. The unlisted pages are not linked from the public site, carry a noindex header and are left out of the sitemap, but anyone with the link can open them.
