off-sf-fidiKey — What the Terms Mean
Levered IRRAnnualized return on the equity invested, after debt, accounting for the timing of every cash flow.
Unlevered IRRThe same annualized return measured on an all-cash basis, with no loan.
Equity MultipleTotal dollars returned to equity divided by dollars invested — 2.0× means the money doubled.
Total ProfitCumulative cash returned to equity over the hold, above the capital invested.
Hold PeriodThe number of years the asset is owned before it is sold.
NOI (Net Operating Income)Rental income plus expense recoveries, minus operating expenses — before debt and capital costs.
Going-in YieldYear-one NOI divided by total cost; the initial income return on the purchase.
Cap RateNOI divided by value — the income yield used to price the asset.
Exit Cap RateThe cap rate applied to forward NOI at sale to estimate the future sale price.
Year-1 DSCRNOI divided by debt service in year one; the lender’s coverage cushion (above 1.0×, income covers the loan).
Debt ServiceThe periodic interest — and principal, if amortizing — paid on the loan.
Capital StackThe layers of capital funding the deal: senior debt, then any mezzanine, then equity.
Senior / MezzanineSenior debt is the first-lien loan; mezzanine is subordinate debt between senior debt and equity.
Sources & UsesWhere the capital comes from (sources) and what it pays for (uses) at closing.
Sensitivity (Tornado)How much the IRR swings when each assumption is shocked up and down — longer bars matter more.
Pre-Mortem / Kill PointHow far one assumption must move against you to drop the return to your failure threshold.
WALTWeighted-average lease term remaining across in-place tenants, in years.
