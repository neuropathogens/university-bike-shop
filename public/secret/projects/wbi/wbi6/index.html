X. Uncertainty — Per-Assumption Errors

Toggle an error on to make that assumption random in the simulation; it is drawn around the value you entered above, in the same units. Normal: first number is the standard deviation (about 2-in-3 of draws land within it). Uniform: first number is the maximum swing, equally likely anywhere within ±. Triangular: first number is the most it could fall below your value, second the most it could rise above. Lognormal: first number is sigma on the log scale; small misses are common, large overshoots rare but possible.
