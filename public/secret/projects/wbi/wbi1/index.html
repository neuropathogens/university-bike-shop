Due diligence

Verify before going hard
  • Electrical (amp/volt): licensed electrician to confirm service vs. seller's claim — inflated = $50K–$500K+ upgrade.
  • Two on-site transformers: confirm ownership, age, kVA — test for PCBs (pre-1979 likely; customer-owned = your liability).
  • Repave ~1/4 of lot + signage: in the capex slider. Buildings reportedly sound — confirm roof/structural/seismic.
  • Title & survey: easements, access, parcel lines, 10.11 ac.
Environmental = the catch
