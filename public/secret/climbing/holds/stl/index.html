Abstract

This supplement to grip-lab: A Parametric Kit of 3D-Printed Grip and Finger Trainers for Bouldering collects the STL file for every printed piece of the kit, 50 files in all (about 168 MB). Only printed pieces are listed. The steel (threaded rod, shafts, nuts, washers, eye nuts) and the electronics are bought, not printed: see the bill of materials in Section 11.2 of the paper. Every file was exported from the same checked models as the paper's figures.

Before you print

  • Orientation is baked in. Each file sits on the bed (Z = 0) in its print orientation. Do not rotate it in the slicer: the orientation keeps every load in the layer plane.
  • Units are millimetres. If a part imports 25× too large or small, the slicer guessed inches.
  • Settings: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 6 perimeters, 4 mm solid top and bottom, 40 % gyroid infill, no supports.
  • Materials: load pieces (“PA-CF”) in PA6-CF or PA12-CF (hardened nozzle), PETG at minimum. Never PLA for anything you hang from. TPU 95A only where listed.
  • Qty is how many of that file one trainer needs. Where a table offers choices (cassettes, jaw faces, collar and sleeve sizes), its caption says what to pick.
  • Proof-test every load piece at 1.5× its rating for 30 s before use, and retire it at the first whitening, creak, crack or set.
