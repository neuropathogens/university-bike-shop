universitybikeshop.com

Summary

Everything that has changed on the University Bikes website since it first went up in June 2026, newest first. The site has had three lives: hand-uploaded pages in June; a static site with a listings back end (the universitybikes repository) from late June; and the current Next.js site (university-bike-shop) since July 30, which every change from August onward builds on. Entries come from the commit history and pull requests of both repositories and the Netlify deploy log.

Era 3 · Next.js site

July 30, 2026 – present · repository neuropathogens/university-bike-shop

It started as an empty shell and grew tab by tab: live Google Sheets for inventory and repair prices, Summer Camp, About Us, Sponsorships, Bike Fits, Sell Your Bike and Location & Hours. Era 2 · Static site with a listings back end

June 28 – July 4, 2026 · repository neuropathogens/universitybikes

A hand-built static site served from Netlify's CDN, with small serverless functions and Netlify storage behind it so bikes for sale could be listed, photographed and published without a server. It was a contact-funnel site: nothing was sold online, and every path led to a call or text.

  1. Jul 4Admin guide
    • Written guide to managing listings: statuses, importing, photos, publishing, featuring and troubleshooting.
  2. Jul 1Logo and team titles
    • New chainring logo and site icons, used in the navigation and menus.
    • Playful new team titles; homepage styles cleaned up.
  3. Jul 1Homepage rebuild and polish
    • New homepage: live inventory counter, "Just In" featured bikes, how-it-works band, sell teaser and contact call to action.
    • Accessibility: skip link, keyboard focus handling, labelled galleries.
    • Search: structured data for the shop and each bike, share previews, icons, auto-generated sitemap of bikes for sale, security headers.
    • Fuller Repairs page; charity wording corrected to supporting Silicon Valley Bike Exchange through parts purchases.
  4. Jun 28Site launched from a repository
    • Pages: home, bikes for sale with a page per bike, repairs, sell your bike, team (12 people in four departments) and a 404 page.
    • Shop email on every page and a date-aware summer-break banner.
    • Behind the scenes: listings and photos stored on Netlify, with a tool that turns a saved Facebook Marketplace listing into a bike-for-sale page in one click.

Era 1 · First pages

June 21 – 22, 2026 · manual uploads

The Netlify site for universitybikeshop.com was created on June 21, and the first versions were published by dragging a folder into Netlify, with no repository behind them. Six versions went up over two days. There are no commit messages, so their contents aren't recorded here.
