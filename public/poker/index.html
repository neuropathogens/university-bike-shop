Live Hold’em Tables

Memorization tables for no-limit hold’em, 5–9 players

Two companion papers for beginners at live tables. The first turns every decision into a lookup: find the spot, then read one cell of one table. The strategy is deliberately simple; it is built to avoid the large mistakes that cost beginners the most, not to beat strong players. The second covers what the cards do not: what your body gives away, how to read opponents, and how to keep playing the tables under stress. Read them in order.

1Algorithmic Live Hold’em: Memorization Tables for 5–9 Player GamesAll 169 starting hands in five tiers by position and action; seven postflop hand classes and four board textures, with bet sizes; pot odds, outs, stack-to-pot ratio and multiway fold equity11 pp170 KB
2Composure and Deception at the Live Table: Mental-Game Tables for Hold’emOne physical routine for every moment of a hand; opponent tells ranked by reliability; table image and timing versus angle shooting; tilt and breathing protocols, with a six-week drill program11 pp130 KB

Published drafts, November 2026. Free to use for personal study.
